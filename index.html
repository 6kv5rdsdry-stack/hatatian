Our Story

A private jeweler
rooted in Mombasa.

Hatatian Jewelry was born from a singular belief: that Africa's earth holds some of the most extraordinary gemstones in the world — and that those stones deserve to be treated with integrity, artistry, and reverence from the moment they leave the ground.

"We don't sell jewelry. We create heirlooms — one stone, one person, one story at a time."

As a private jeweler operating by appointment, every relationship begins with a conversation. There is no mass catalog to browse. Instead, we sit with you via virtual consultations or direct inquiries, understand your vision, and build something that outlives the moment it was made for.

Ethical Sourcing

Mine to Market Traceability.

We work directly with independent artisanal miners across East Africa. Every gemstone in our collection carries a verifiable origin story, ensuring fair labor and community empowerment at the source.

"True luxury honors both the craft and the hands that brought the mineral from the earth."
