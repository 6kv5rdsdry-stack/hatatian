Our Story

A private jeweler
rooted in Nairobi.

Hatatian Jewelry was born from a singular belief: that Africa's earth holds some of the most extraordinary gemstones in the world — and that those stones deserve to be treated with integrity, artistry, and reverence from the moment they leave the ground.

"We don't sell jewelry. We create heirlooms — one stone, one person, one story at a time."

As a private jeweler operating by appointment, every relationship begins with a conversation. There is no catalogue to browse, no shelf to scan. Instead, we sit with you, understand your story, and build something that will outlive the moment it was made for.

The Inspiration Behind the Green Garnet Collection
The Tsavo Green Collection takes its name and its soul from Kenya's Tsavo landscape — one of the most ancient, untouched wilderness regions on earth. The green of a Tsavorite garnet at its finest is not the green of emerald, which can feel composed and arranged. It is wilder than that. More alive. It carries the specific quality of light that falls through East African canopy at six in the morning — layered, saturated, and impossible to replicate anywhere else on the planet. Every piece in this collection is designed to let that green speak without interference. Minimal settings. Maximum stone. Tsavorite that is certified unenhanced, sourced from Taita Taveta, and set in 18k yellow gold — the only metal warm enough to hold it.

The Gemstones
Hatatian Gems

Stones with
stories older
than time.

Kenya and East Africa sit atop one of the most geologically remarkable regions on earth. The same ancient forces that shaped the Great Rift Valley also crystallised some of the world's rarest gems. We source them with care, trace them with integrity, and transform them with artistry.

Enquire About a Stone
Tsavorite Garnet
Taita Taveta, Kenya · Unenhanced · Untreated

The rarest of garnets — a vivid green of extraordinary saturation and brilliance. Found only in the arc stretching from Tanzania through Kenya, tsavorite is prized above emerald by those who know stones. Ours are sourced directly from Taita Taveta, unenhanced, and certified.

Rarity
Teal Sapphire
Garbatula, Kenya · Untreated

Where blue meets green in a single stone. Teal sapphires shift with the light — from deep ocean at dusk to bright sky at noon. No two are alike, and their character only deepens over time.

Rarity
Dragon Garnet
Tanzania · Colour-change

Dragon Garnet shifts from mauve in daylight to fiery red under incandescent light — one of the most coveted colour-change phenomena in gemology. A stone that is never the same stone twice.

Rarity
Spinel
Tanzania · No Heat Treatment

Tanzanian Spinels span an extraordinary range of colour — from pinks and reds to rare cobalt blues — all unified by a natural brilliance that requires no heat treatment to achieve.

Rarity
Aquamarine
Kenya · Pale Blue

The blue of still water at first light. Kenyan aquamarine is known for its exceptional transparency and gentle, unwavering colour — a stone of remarkable calm.

Rarity
Ethical Sourcing
Fully Traced · Mine to Atelier

Every Hatatian Gems stone arrives with a clear trace of its journey — who found it, where, and how. We work only with mining partners who protect both the land and the livelihoods of local communities.

Our Promise
05
Ethics & Origin
Stewardship

We are stewards,
not just sellers.

Africa has long watched its most precious resources leave its borders with too little benefit returned to the communities that uncovered them. Hatatian Jewelry was founded to be different.

"The earth gives. We are obligated to give back with the same generosity."

We source directly. We pay fairly. We trace openly. And we tell the full story of every stone — because you deserve to know exactly what you are wearing, and who made it possible.
