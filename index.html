Our Story & Promise
Created as a daughter's promise to honor her late mother, Mama Carol.
Founder's Heritage

A legacy born of
love and light.

Hatatian Jewelry was founded by Hanly Mutsune as an eternal tribute to her late mother, Mama Carol. Every gemstone we source and every setting we design carries forward a standard of uncompromising grace, deep integrity, and authentic personal connection.

“We do not simply set stones; we curate legacies that honor where they came from.”

Operating as a private consultancy and bespoke jeweler, we work exclusively with independent artisanal miners across East Africa to ensure every piece is ethically sourced, fully traceable, and uniquely yours.
