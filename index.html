Our Story

A private jeweler
born in Kenya.

Hatatian Jewelry was born from a singular belief: that Africa's earth holds some of the most extraordinary gemstones in the world — and that those stones deserve to be treated with integrity, artistry, and reverence from the moment they leave the ground.

"We don't sell jewelry. We create heirlooms — artfully crafted in Kenya, one stone, one person, one story at a time."

As a private jeweler operating by appointment, every relationship begins with a conversation. There is no catalogue to browse, no shelf to scan. Instead, we sit with you, understand your story, and build something that will outlive the moment it was made for.
